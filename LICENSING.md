## Changing the license

A change to copyleft (for example AGPL-3.0) needs the rights holders' authority
for the affected code. Do not relicense third-party code as Clawd-owned. Prior
MIT grants for copies already distributed remain relevant; a new license
cannot make those copies private. The maintainer must settle the license and
ownership of proposed release content before publication.

Contributors retain their copyrights and must have permission to submit their
work under the applicable component license. Disclose imported source and
preserve its notices. Do not contribute third-party proprietary material,
provider credentials, private datasets, or wallet secrets. The contribution
process does not assign copyright to OpenClawd.
